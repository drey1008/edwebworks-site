05FAQ

Questions

How much does a website cost?

Basic is from $799 and Growth is from $1,499. Custom is quoted on request, and Care is an optional monthly add on at $49 a month. All prices are excl. VAT, so send me a message and you will get a clear, fixed price before we begin.

What is included in the price?

Setting up your site is included. Domain and hosting are covered by the Care add on, so you do not pay for them anywhere else. You can use your own logo and photos. If you do not have a logo, I can design one for you by agreement. Photography can be arranged separately.

Do you work with businesses outside Denmark?

Yes. I am based in Copenhagen, Denmark, and I work remotely, so it does not matter where your business is. We can talk by phone or video, and you follow the progress through a link.

Do I have to pay for the demo?

No. The demo is free and there is no commitment. You see what your site could look like, and only if you want to continue do we agree on a package and a price.

How fast can my site be ready?

Usually within a few days once I have your copy, prices and photos. You get a status update every week along the way.

What kinds of businesses do you build for?

All kinds, big and small. Smaller shops that often get overlooked are especially welcome. Hair and beauty salons are my main focus, but I also build for cafés, tradespeople and clinics.

Can you help me get found on Google?

Every site is built to be fast and mobile friendly. With Growth I also write SEO copy. Nobody can promise a specific ranking on Google, but a good site makes it easier for customers to find you.

Are the example sites in English?

The four example sites are in Danish, because they were made for the Danish market. Your site will be written in the language your customers speak.
